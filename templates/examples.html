Step‑by‑Step Worked Examples

Example 1: \( \int_0^3 x^2 \, dx \)

Exact value: \( \left[ \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^3 = 9 \).

Let \( n = 6 \) (even). Then \( h = \frac{3-0}{6} = 0.5 \).

Nodes: \( x_0=0,\; x_1=0.5,\; x_2=1,\; x_3=1.5,\; x_4=2,\; x_5=2.5,\; x_6=3 \).

\( f(x)=x^2 \):

  • \( f(x_0)+f(x_6) = 0^2 + 3^2 = 9 \)
  • Odd indices (1,3,5): \( 4[ (0.5)^2 + (1.5)^2 + (2.5)^2 ] = 4[0.25 + 2.25 + 6.25] = 4 \times 8.75 = 35 \)
  • Even indices (2,4): \( 2[ (1)^2 + (2)^2 ] = 2[1+4] = 10 \)

Sum: \( 9 + 35 + 10 = 54 \)

Approximation: \( \frac{h}{3} \times 54 = \frac{0.5}{3} \times 54 = 0.166666\ldots \times 54 = 9 \)

Result: 9 (exact, because Simpson's rule integrates polynomials of degree ≤3 exactly).

Example 2: \( \int_0^\pi \sin(x) \, dx \)

Exact value: \( [-\cos(x)]_0^\pi = 2 \).

Take \( n = 4 \), so \( h = \frac{\pi - 0}{4} = \pi/4 \approx 0.785398 \).

Nodes: \( x_0=0,\; x_1=\pi/4,\; x_2=\pi/2,\; x_3=3\pi/4,\; x_4=\pi \).

\( f(x)=\sin x \):

  • \( f(x_0)+f(x_4) = \sin0 + \sin\pi = 0 + 0 = 0 \)
  • Odd indices (1,3): \( 4[ \sin(\pi/4) + \sin(3\pi/4) ] = 4[0.70710678 + 0.70710678] \)
          \(= 4 \times 1.41421356 = 5.65685424 \)
  • Even index (2): \( 2 \times \sin(\pi/2) = 2 \times 1 = 2 \)

Sum: \( 0 + 5.65685424 + 2 = 7.65685424 \)

Approximation: \( \frac{h}{3} \times 7.65685424 = \frac{0.785398}{3} \times 7.65685424 \approx 0.261799 \times 7.65685424 \approx 2.005 \)

Very close to exact value 2 (error ~0.005).
