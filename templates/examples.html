Step‑by‑Step Worked Examples

Example 1: \( \int_0^3 x^2 \, dx \)

Step 1: Exact value (analytical):

\[ \int_0^3 x^2 \, dx = \left[ \frac{x^3}{3} \right]_0^3 = \frac{27}{3} - 0 = 9 \]

Step 2: Choose \( n = 6 \) (even). Compute step size \( h \):

\[ h = \frac{b - a}{n} = \frac{3 - 0}{6} = 0.5 \]

Step 3: List the nodes:

\[ x_0 = 0,\; x_1 = 0.5,\; x_2 = 1,\; x_3 = 1.5,\; x_4 = 2,\; x_5 = 2.5,\; x_6 = 3 \]

Step 4: Evaluate \( f(x) = x^2 \) at each node:

\[ f(x_0)=0,\; f(x_1)=0.25,\; f(x_2)=1,\; f(x_3)=2.25,\;\] \[f(x_4)=4,\; f(x_5)=6.25,\; f(x_6)=9 \]

Step 5: Apply Simpson's 1/3 rule formula:

\[ \int_a^b f(x)\,dx \approx \frac{h}{3}\left[ f(x_0) + f(x_6) + 4\sum_{\text{odd}} f(x_i) + 2\sum_{\text{even}} f(x_i) \right] \]

  • \( f(x_0) + f(x_6) = 0 + 9 = 9 \)
  • Odd indices (1,3,5): \( 4 \times (0.25 + 2.25 + 6.25) = 4 \times 8.75 = 35 \)
  • Even indices (2,4): \( 2 \times (1 + 4) = 2 \times 5 = 10 \)

Step 6: Sum all contributions:

\[ S = 9 + 35 + 10 = 54 \]

Step 7: Multiply by \( \frac{h}{3} = \frac{0.5}{3} = 0.166666\ldots \):

\[ \text{Approximation} = 0.166666\ldots \times 54 = 9 \]

Result: \( 9 \) (exact, because Simpson's rule integrates polynomials of degree ≤3 exactly).

Example 2: \( \int_0^\pi \sin(x) \, dx \)

Step 1: Exact value (analytical):

\[ \int_0^\pi \sin(x) \, dx = \left[ -\cos(x) \right]_0^\pi = (-\cos\pi) - (-\cos 0)\] \[= (-(-1)) - (-1) = 1 + 1 = 2 \]

Step 2: Choose \( n = 4 \) (even). Compute step size \( h \):

\[ h = \frac{\pi - 0}{4} = \frac{\pi}{4} \approx 0.7853981634 \]

Step 3: List the nodes:

\[ x_0 = 0,\; x_1 = \pi/4 \approx 0.785398,\; x_2 = \pi/2 \approx 1.570796,\; \] \[x_3 = 3\pi/4 \approx 2.356194,\; x_4 = \pi \approx 3.141593 \]

Step 4: Evaluate \( f(x) = \sin(x) \) at each node:

\[ \begin{aligned} f(x_0) &= \sin(0) = 0 \\ f(x_1) &= \sin(\pi/4) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \approx 0.70710678 \\ f(x_2) &= \sin(\pi/2) = 1 \\ f(x_3) &= \sin(3\pi/4) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \approx 0.70710678 \\ f(x_4) &= \sin(\pi) = 0 \end{aligned} \]

Step 5: Apply Simpson's 1/3 rule formula:

\[ \int_a^b f(x)\,dx \approx \frac{h}{3}\left[ f(x_0) + f(x_4) + 4\sum_{\text{odd}} f(x_i) + 2\sum_{\text{even}} f(x_i) \right] \]

  • \( f(x_0) + f(x_4) = 0 + 0 = 0 \)
  • Odd indices (1,3): \( 4 \times (0.70710678 + 0.70710678) = 4 \times 1.41421356 = 5.65685424 \)
  • Even index (2): \( 2 \times 1 = 2 \)

Step 6: Sum all contributions:

\[ S = 0 + 5.65685424 + 2 = 7.65685424 \]

Step 7: Multiply by \( \frac{h}{3} = \frac{0.7853981634}{3} \approx 0.2617993878 \):

\[ \text{Approximation} = 0.2617993878 \times 7.65685424 \approx 2.005 \]

Result: \( \approx 2.005 \) (very close to exact value 2, error ≈ 0.005).
